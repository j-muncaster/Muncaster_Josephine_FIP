Health & Ingredients

Orbitz is made with your wellness in mind.

Sparkling Mineral Water

Orbitz begins with clean, mineral-rich water—sourced for purity and balance. These naturally occurring minerals, like calcium, magnesium, and potassium, help support hydration, bone health, and energy production. Unlike purified or distilled waters, mineral water contains trace elements that nourish your body on a cellular level. It’s a crisp, refreshing base that brings every flavor of Orbitz to life.

Organic Fruit

Each bottle of Orbitz is infused with real organic fruit, chosen for both taste and function. From tangy raspberry to mellow melon, our fruity blends are made without artificial sweeteners or synthetic flavors—just clean, certified organic ingredients. These fruits offer natural antioxidants, subtle sweetness, and a vibrant experience with every sip. It’s nature’s flavor, exactly as it should be.

Chicory Root Fibre

Chicory root fibre is one of nature’s most effective prebiotics, and a cornerstone ingredient in Orbitz. Extracted from the root of the chicory plant, this soluble fibre feeds the beneficial bacteria in your gut, helping to support digestion and maintain microbiome balance. It’s gentle, plant-based, and shown to improve regularity and gut health over time. Plus, it blends seamlessly into our sparkling flavors—so all you taste is refreshment.

Prebiotics

Orbitz delivers a daily dose of prebiotics to help you feel balanced from the inside out. Prebiotics act as fuel for your body’s good bacteria, promoting digestive wellness, improved immunity, and better nutrient absorption. Unlike probiotics, prebiotics are more shelf-stable and consistent, offering gut support without needing refrigeration. With Orbitz, you’re nourishing your microbiome in every bubbly sip.

Our Story

Our Story

Born from Balance. Crafted by Women. Rooted in Wellness.
Orbitz began as a simple idea: what if feeling good could feel joyful, fizzy, and full of nature’s brilliance?
Created by women who wanted more from their daily drink—a blend of nourishment, energy, and elegance—Orbitz was imagined as a ritual of restoration. Something that didn’t just hydrate, but supported the whole self: gut, mood, body, and spirit.
Backed by science and inspired by nature, each bottle combines prebiotic fiber with organic botanicals and fruity effervescence to help women bloom through busy days, cycles, and seasons.
We believe wellness should orbit around you—your flow, your pace, your glow. With every sip of Orbitz, you’re not just drinking—you’re thriving from within. It’s wellness in motion.
